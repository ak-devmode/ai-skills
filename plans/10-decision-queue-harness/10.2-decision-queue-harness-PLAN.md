## Phase 2: Review re-pointed at decisions

Rewritten in CEO review (scope §4.2). All of it starts today in its own ai-skills worktree,
concurrent with 10.1; Task 2.5 waits for 10.1's `packet.py`.

### Task 2.1: Findings budget
- **Type**: AI
- **Input**: `review/scripts/review.py`, `scripts/verdict-gate.py`, review SKILL.md §5.1, baseline §3.1
- **Action**: round 1 uncapped (fix every finding that makes sense); from round 2 blocking always fixed, should-fix capped at the reviewer's top few, notes logged with at most one batch fix and no re-review; allow `accepted` on notes at any round
- **Output**: review.py, verdict-gate.py, SKILL.md §5.1
- **Acceptance**: tests: round-1 cap absent; round-2 should-fix over the cap → refused as extra work, not required; note `accepted` at round 1 passes the gate

### Task 2.2: Reframe prompt + fresh judge
- **Type**: AI
- **Input**: review prompt, 2.1
- **Action**: every round the reviewer states whether a simpler design removes several findings; a fresh judge via `scripts/codex-exec.py` (objective + findings + proposal, plus scope §11, the Decisions Log and the lineage's earlier rejected reframes) accepts only if it meets the plan objective and simplifies, naming what it deletes; rejected → one-line reason recorded; the judge also flags findings that re-open a recorded Alex decision; fallback `local-maxima` flags advisory
- **Output**: review prompt, SKILL.md, review.py records
- **Acceptance**: fixture: a reframe that deletes nothing is rejected with a reason; one that meets the objective and deletes a module is accepted

### Task 2.3: `--reframed` disposition + lineage check (TO-DOs)
- **Type**: AI
- **Input**: ai-skills TO-DOs (`[CASCADE]` outcome, `review_lineage`)
- **Action**: FIRST, before 2.1: fix `review_lineage` (`share = base == phead` continues a lane's lineage into its next unit, so new code counts as round 4–5). Then `dispose --reframed "<what changed>" --commit <sha> --by <judge|user>` at any round, gate treats it like a deferral; the replacing commit is reviewed as a new lineage
- **Output**: review.py, verdict-gate.py
- **Acceptance**: tests per the TO-DO text; lineage TO-DO closed or narrowed with a cited commit

### Task 2.4: Round-1 effort trial
- **Type**: AI
- **Input**: `verify_lib.effort_for`
- **Action**: one tier up on a lineage's first review, behind a flag the scorecard can compare
- **Output**: verify_lib.py, review.py
- **Acceptance**: prepare prints the bumped effort on round 1 only

### Task 2.5: Packets from review and verify
- **Type**: AI
- **Input**: 2.2, 10.1 `packet.py`
- **Action**: the three review cases (accepted reframe overturning a recorded decision or touching a one-way door; blocking finding surviving two rounds with no qualifying reframe; a rejected reframe proposed again) and `/verify`'s run-cap STOP emit packets via the driver; inline only when no queue URL
- **Output**: review.py, verify SKILL.md §3.9
- **Acceptance**: a fixture for each case produces a packet JSON matching the schema

### Task 2.R: Review
- **Type**: AI
- **Action**: `/review` on each repo's `base..HEAD` (base from `closeout-prep.md`), with
  `--scope`/`--unit 10.2`; disposition every finding ID (/plan §6.8)
- **Acceptance**: range non-empty; every finding `fixed <sha>`, `rejected <reason>`, `accepted "<nit>"`
  or, past round 3 of its lineage, `deferred "<TO-DO item>"`; on a SHIP,
  `review.py accept --by "<owner> (standing policy)"` (/review §5.1)
