### 3.1 Round depth — corrected (`lineage.py`, 2026-10-25)

The first pass counted rounds by the unit's `r<n>` id. The gate does not: `review.py`
`review_round()` counts per **lineage** (a fix re-review continues, new code starts at r1), and
one unit spans many repos and lanes. Re-counted with the gate's own functions over 86 units:

| Lineage round | Reviews | Findings/rev | Blocking/rev |
|---|---|---|---|
| r1 | 291 | 7.7 | 1.23 |
| r2 | 153 | 6.2 | 1.14 |
| r3 | 99 | 5.7 | 1.17 |
| r4 | 43 | 4.0 | 0.77 |
| r5 | 20 | 3.1 | 0.75 |
| r6+ | 15 | 3.9 | 1.60 |

- **13% of reviews are at lineage round ≥4** (first pass said 64%). Volume is breadth: 291
  lineages, ~2.1 reviews each. Depth is the minority.
- Blocking per review holds ~1.2 through r3, then drops to ~0.75. Loops do converge, but slowly.
- Late-round findings (r≥4) still land on an already-hit file 58–66% of the time.
- `local-maxima` flags: **fallback 120 in 178 reviews, codex 11 in 398, lean 12 in 45**. The
  flag is mostly the Claude fallback reviewer's labelling habit, not a sol property. After a
  flag the lineage ran a median ~3 more rounds (first pass said 10).
- The round-3 stop already exists: `review.py convergence()` emits a STOP at `ROUND_CAP` 3 with
  no SHIP (since 2026-10-18), plus a repeated-file "raise as design finding" stop. Part of this
  window predates it.

### 3.2 Where later-round findings land (`infix.py`, 2026-10-26)

Each finding's `file:line` against its own review's diff hunks (±3 lines). A fix-only range
starts at an earlier review's head in the same repo; a wide one re-reviews the original too.

| Reviewer · round | Reviews | Findings/rev | In the range's hunks | Same file, outside | Other file |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| codex r1 | 178 | 3.8 | 91% | 7% | 3% |
| codex r2+ fix-only | 179 | 2.5 | 80% | 15% | 5% |
| codex r2+ wide | 41 | 4.6 | 88% | 9% | 3% |
| fallback r1 | 79 | 18.0 | 84% | 11% | 5% |
| fallback r2+ fix-only | 87 | 10.0 | 79% | 17% | 4% |

- **Later rounds review the fixes, not missed code.** 81% of codex's later reviews are
  fix-only ranges, and 80% of their findings sit on lines the fix itself wrote. At most the
  ~20% outside the hunks could have surfaced in round 1.
- Not separable without reading the text: a finding on a fix's line may be the original defect
  not fully fixed, or a new one the fix introduced.

Superseded first-pass claims (unit-level counting, do not cite): "64% are a unit's 4th+
review", "flat ~5 findings with ~1 blocking indefinitely", "median 10 more reviews after a
local-maxima fix".
